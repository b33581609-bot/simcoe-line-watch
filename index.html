How this works and where it can be wrong

The county outline is the geographic Simcoe County from Land Information Ontario, so Barrie and Orillia are inside it. Georgian Bay islands such as Christian Island are not drawn. The outline is generalized along the shore, so waterfront spots can read as just outside.

The warning band is 1.5 km plus your current GPS accuracy. Inside that band the banner turns amber and the page cannot say for sure which side you are on.

The Williams Treaties outline is the 1923 territory as generalized by Indigenous Services Canada. It is not a map of where you may fish. Earlier treaty lands, such as Peterborough and the Kawarthas, are left out of it.

The street map is OpenStreetMap, loaded over the internet, so you need data or wifi for it. The county and treaty outlines and the status banner still work without it.

Alerts: a tone and a vibration (vibration works on Android only) when you move toward or past the county line, repeated every 30 seconds while you are outside. No position is saved or sent anywhere. The trail disappears when you close the page.
